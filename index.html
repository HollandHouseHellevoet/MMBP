Section 2 of 4: Deal Summary

MedMerge

Company

MedMerge provides health benefits administration for small and rural hospitals. The company built a single employer health plan chassis that gives each hospital its own fully compliant, self-funded health plan with transparent pricing and direct savings. The chassis took 18 months to build and is live in three states.

The Problem

Small hospitals with fewer than 500 lives are trapped in fully insured arrangements where carriers control pricing, network access, and plan design. These employers overpay by 15 to 30 percent compared to self-funded alternatives, but lack the infrastructure to administer their own plans. No one is building for this market because each customer is small. MedMerge makes it work by running one chassis across many employers.

How to Read This

The cap is what matters most. At a $40M cap, investors own 7.50% of the company at conversion. At a $50M cap, they own 6.00%. That 1.50 percentage point difference translates to $1,125,000 at a $75M Series A and $1,500,000 at a $100M Series A. In every scenario, the cap delivers a better price than the 20% discount, which means the cap is the binding term.

The coupon pays $720,000 in cash over three years regardless of what happens at conversion. That is a 24% cash return on invested capital before any equity upside.
