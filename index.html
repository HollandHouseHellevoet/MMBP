Conversion Shares
Shares of the series of Preferred Stock issued in the Equity Financing, on the same terms and with the same rights, preferences, and privileges
Most Favored Nation
MFN Provision
If the Company issues any subsequent SAFE or convertible instrument with terms more favorable to the holder than the terms of this SAFE, the Company shall promptly notify the Investor and, at the Investor's election, amend this SAFE to incorporate such more favorable terms
Liquidity and Dissolution
Liquidity Event
Upon a Change of Control or Direct Listing, the Investor receives the greater of (a) the Purchase Amount or (b) the amount payable based on conversion at the Valuation Cap
Dissolution Event
Upon dissolution, wind-up, or liquidation, the Investor receives the Purchase Amount, paid out of remaining assets in preference to holders of Common Stock and on par with other SAFE holders
