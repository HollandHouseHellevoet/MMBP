20% discount to the price per share paid by new investors in the Equity Financing
Conversion Price
The lower of (a) the price per share based on the Valuation Cap and (b) the Discount Price. The Investor receives the more favorable conversion.
Equity Financing
A bona fide transaction or series of transactions with the principal purpose of raising capital, pursuant to which the Company issues and sells Preferred Stock at a fixed pre-money valuation
Conversion Shares
Shares of the series of Preferred Stock issued in the Equity Financing, on the same terms and with the same rights, preferences, and privileges
Most Favored Nation
MFN Provision
If the Company issues any subsequent SAFE or convertible instrument with terms more favorable to the holder than the terms of this SAFE, the Company shall promptly notify the Investor and, at the Investor's election, amend this SAFE to incorporate such more favorable terms
Liquidity and Dissolution
Liquidity Event

Upon a Change of Control or Direct Listing, the Investor receives the greater of (a) the Purchase Amount or (b) the amount payable based on conversion at the Valuation Cap

Dissolution Event
Upon dissolution, wind-up, or liquidation, the Investor receives the Purchase Amount, paid out of remaining assets in preference to holders of Common Stock and on par with other SAFE holders
General Provisions
Pro Rata Rights
The Investor shall have the right, but not the obligation, to participate in the Equity Financing on a pro rata basis based on the Investor's ownership percentage on an as-converted basis
Information Rights
The Company shall provide annual financial statements and a brief written update on operations to all SAFE holders within 90 days of each fiscal year end
