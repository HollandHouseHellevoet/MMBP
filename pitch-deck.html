The Problem

Small and rural hospitals face a benefits crisis that no one is solving.

  • Health plan costs are rising 8 to 12 percent annually for employers with fewer than 500 lives
  • Fully insured carriers dictate pricing, network access, and plan design with no transparency
  • Self-funded alternatives require infrastructure that small hospitals do not have
  • The result: these employers overpay, lose employees, and have no leverage to change it

What MedMerge Built

A single employer health plan chassis, purpose-built for small hospitals.

  • Fully compliant plan structure that took 18 months to design and build
  • Each employer gets its own plan with transparent pricing, no pooled risk with strangers
  • MedMerge handles administration, compliance, and network access
  • Employers keep savings when claims come in lower than expected
