Conversion at Series A

At a priced round, the SAFE converts at the lower of the valuation cap or a 20% discount to the Series A price. The cap determines how much equity investors receive. A lower cap means more equity for the same dollars invested.

How to Read This

The cap is what matters most. At a $40M cap, investors own 7.50% of the company at conversion. At a $50M cap, they own 6.00%. That 1.50 percentage point difference translates to $1,125,000 at a $75M Series A and $1,500,000 at a $100M Series A. In every scenario, the cap delivers a better price than the 20% discount, which means the cap is the binding term.

The coupon pays $720,000 in cash over three years regardless of what happens at conversion. That is a 24% cash return on invested capital before any equity upside.
